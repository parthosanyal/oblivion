    About the ashtakavarga

    Ashtakavarga is a points system. Each of the seven planets and the Lagna gives or withholds a bindu (point) to each sign, depending on classical rules of position. The result shows which signs and houses are well supported in your chart.

    The Bhinnashtakavarga is the chart of one planet; the Sarvashtakavarga adds all of them. AstroSarangi draws both, with bar views and a full table, and uses them for judging transits.

    How to read it

    In the Sarvashtakavarga, a house with 28 or more points is considered well supported, and one with fewer than 25 is weak. When a slow planet transits a sign with many points, results tend to be easier; in a sign with few points, they ask for more effort.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is a bindu?

    A single point given by a planet to a sign under the Ashtakavarga rules.

    What is a good Sarvashtakavarga score?

    Roughly 28 or more for a house is good; below 25 is weak. The total across all signs is 337.

    How is it used in transits?

    The bindus of a planet in the sign it transits show how strong that transit will be.
