    What Cancer stands for

    Cancer, or Karka, is the fourth sign, symbolised by the Crab. It is a water sign of the movable type, so it begins emotional activity, such as caring for others or building a home. The traditional body areas are the chest and stomach.

    The Moon rules Cancer, the planet of the mind, mother and nourishment. This is the only sign ruled by the Moon alone, and it explains why Cancer is linked with home, family, memory and feeling. The sign is the natural fourth house, the house of home and mother.

    The crab's hard shell is the usual image: Cancer protects itself and those it loves, and can withdraw when hurt. Its moods follow the Moon, rising and falling with circumstance.

    Sun, Moon and rising sign in Cancer

    The Sun in Cancer is read as a character that draws strength from family and roots rather than from rank. Authority is soft and protective. It is a friendly sign for the Sun, since the Moon is the Sun's friend.

    The Moon in its own sign is strong and comfortable. A Cancer Moon feels deeply, remembers everything and needs a safe base. It spans the last quarter of Punarvasu, all of Pushya, and all of Ashlesha, so the birth Dasha is Jupiter, Saturn or Mercury.

    Cancer rising makes the Moon the first-house lord. Such people tend to have a soft, round face and a changing appearance linked to the Moon's phases. Mars rules the fifth and tenth houses and so becomes a yogakaraka, giving it special strength in this chart.

    Career and money

    Cancer suits caring and nourishing professions: nursing, medicine, teaching young children, hospitality, food, real estate, childcare, counselling and public service. Trades linked to water and to the home, such as shipping, dairy and hotels, also fit the sign.

    Financially Cancer is careful, and it saves for security. Cash flow often rises and falls with the mood of the market or the household.

    Love and marriage

    In love Cancer is devoted, protective and sensitive. It needs reassurance and loyalty, and it remembers slights for a long time. A partner who values home life and who communicates feelings openly suits it best.

    Traditionally Cancer fits well with other water signs, Scorpio and Pisces, and with earth signs Taurus and Virgo. The seventh house and the Moon's nakshatra tell more about real compatibility than the sign.

    Health tendencies

    Cancer rules the chest and stomach, so digestion, acidity and emotional eating are the traditional associations, along with the breasts and fluid balance. Because the Moon governs the mind, emotional stress often shows in the body first.

    Regular meals, calm surroundings and good sleep are the usual suggestions.

    This is traditional symbolism for reflection only. It is not medical advice, so see a qualified doctor for any health concern.

    Cancer and the planets

    The Moon is at home. Jupiter is exalted here, strongest at 5 degrees, which makes Cancer an excellent sign for wisdom, generosity and teaching. Mars is debilitated, weakest at 28 degrees: Mars struggles to act freely in a sign ruled by feeling, though other chart factors can offset this.

    The Sun and Mercury are generally comfortable. Venus and Saturn are mixed. Always check house and aspects before judging a planet.

    The Sun-sign dates and the Western zodiac

    Because the Vedic zodiac is sidereal and about 24 degrees behind the Western one, the Sun is in Cancer (Karka) from about Jul 16 to Aug 16. Most people born in those weeks have a Western Sun sign of Leo, and only those born in the first days, up to about Jul 23, would call themselves Cancer in the Western system. Your Vedic Sun sign, Moon sign and rising sign are best taken from a calculated chart, not from a date table.

    Questions people ask about Cancer

    Which planet rules Cancer in Vedic astrology?

    The Moon (Chandra) rules Cancer, and Cancer is the Moon's only sign.

    Why is Jupiter exalted in Cancer?

    Classical texts name Cancer as Jupiter's sign of exaltation, strongest at 5 degrees. The reason given is that Jupiter's wisdom and generosity work well with the nurturing nature of the sign.

    What are the Vedic dates for Cancer?

    The Sun is in Cancer from about 16 July to 16 August in the sidereal system.

    Which nakshatras fall in Cancer?

    The last quarter of Punarvasu, all of Pushya, and all of Ashlesha.
