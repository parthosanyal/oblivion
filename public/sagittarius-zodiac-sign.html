    What Sagittarius stands for

    Sagittarius, or Dhanu, is the ninth sign, symbolised by the Archer, in some texts a centaur. It is a fire sign of the dual type. The traditional body areas are the hips and thighs, which match the legs of a rider.

    Jupiter rules Sagittarius, the planet of wisdom, teachers, religion, law and good fortune. The sign is the natural ninth house, which covers dharma, higher learning, long journeys, mentors and luck.

    Sagittarius looks toward the horizon. It is honest, enthusiastic and philosophical, and it dislikes being tied down. Its frankness can come across as tactless.

    Sun, Moon and rising sign in Sagittarius

    The Sun in Sagittarius is read as an upright, idealistic nature that carries authority through principle and teaching. It is a friendly sign for the Sun, since Jupiter is the Sun's friend.

    The Moon in Sagittarius gives an optimistic, restless mind that wants freedom and meaning. It spans all of Mula, all of Purva Ashadha, and the first quarter of Uttara Ashadha, so the birth Dasha is Ketu, Venus or the Sun.

    Sagittarius rising makes Jupiter the first-house lord. People are often tall and well-built with an open face. Mars rules the fifth and twelfth houses, and the Sun the ninth house, which gives a strong link between the self, faith and fortune.

    Career and money

    Sagittarius suits work in education, law, publishing, religion, philosophy, travel, import and export, sports, advisory and counselling roles. It also suits any career that involves teaching or guiding others.

    Money tends to come through knowledge and through contacts. The tradition warns about over-optimism and unwise risk, so planning matters more than hope.

    Love and marriage

    In love Sagittarius is honest, adventurous and independent. It needs a partner who shares its curiosity and gives freedom. Commitment can be difficult when it feels restricted, but it is loyal to those who share its values.

    Traditionally Sagittarius pairs well with other fire signs, Aries and Leo, and with air signs Libra and Aquarius. As ever, the seventh house and Moon nakshatra give a better test.

    Health tendencies

    Sagittarius governs the hips, thighs and liver, so sciatica, hip strain and liver or fat metabolism are the traditional associations. Jupiter's excess shows as overeating and weight gain.

    Outdoor movement and moderation in food are the common suggestions.

    This is traditional symbolism for reflection only. It is not medical advice, so see a qualified doctor for any health concern.

    Sagittarius and the planets

    Jupiter is at home, and its moolatrikona is Sagittarius from 0 to 10 degrees. The Sun and Mars are friendly. Saturn and Mercury are mixed, since Mercury prefers detail while Sagittarius prefers the big picture.

    No classical planet is exalted or debilitated in Sagittarius.

    The Sun-sign dates and the Western zodiac

    Because the Vedic zodiac is sidereal and about 24 degrees behind the Western one, the Sun is in Sagittarius (Dhanu) from about Dec 16 to Jan 13. Most people born in those weeks have a Western Sun sign of Capricorn, and only those born in the first days, up to about Dec 22, would call themselves Sagittarius in the Western system. Your Vedic Sun sign, Moon sign and rising sign are best taken from a calculated chart, not from a date table.

    Questions people ask about Sagittarius

    Which planet rules Sagittarius in Vedic astrology?

    Jupiter (Guru) rules Sagittarius and Pisces.

    Is Sagittarius a lucky sign?

    Sagittarius is the natural ninth house, which is the house of fortune, and Jupiter is the planet of good fortune. Actual luck in a chart depends on the ninth house, its lord and the Dasha running.

    What are the Vedic dates for Sagittarius?

    The Sun is in Sagittarius from about 16 December to 13 January in the sidereal system.

    Which nakshatras fall in Sagittarius?

    Mula, Purva Ashadha, and the first quarter of Uttara Ashadha.
