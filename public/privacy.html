Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Free tools

The Kundli, divisional charts, Dasha, Shadbala, Ashtakavarga and Transit tools run in your browser. Your birth details are not sent to our server for these.

Paid and preview reports

When you open a report, your birth details (date, time, place coordinates, time zone, and an optional name) are sent over HTTPS to our server to generate it. They are processed in memory to produce the report and are not stored or written to logs. For Marriage compatibility, the partner details you enter are handled the same way.

Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive only a payment confirmation (order and payment ID), the report type, and a one-way fingerprint of the birth details so access can be tied to that chart. We do not receive your card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay's own privacy policy applies to the payment step.

On your device

We store your last-used form values and your purchase access tokens in your browser's local storage so you do not have to re-enter them. You can clear them by clearing site data.

Place search

When you type a place name, the text is sent to Open-Meteo's free geocoding service to find coordinates.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics.
