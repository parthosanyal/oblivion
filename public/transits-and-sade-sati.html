    About the transits

    Transits, called Gochar, are the present positions of the planets measured against your birth chart. In Vedic astrology they are read mainly from the Moon sign, and they show how the sky is moving over the promises of your Dasha.

    The page shows the transit chart over your Lagna, the transit Navamsa, the strength of each transit by Ashtakavarga points and a plain reading. It also checks Sade Sati, the seven-and-a-half-year period when Saturn passes the 12th, 1st and 2nd signs from your Moon.

    How to read it

    Slow planets matter most: Saturn and Jupiter show the long background, Mars, Sun, Venus and Mercury give shorter triggers. A good transit works best when the running Dasha agrees with it. Sade Sati is not seven years of hardship; its effect depends on Saturn’s place and strength in your chart.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is Gochar?

    The Hindi word for transit, meaning the movement of planets through the signs today compared with your birth chart.

    How long does Sade Sati last?

    About 7.5 years, as Saturn spends roughly 2.5 years in each of the three signs.
