    About the divisional charts

    Divisional charts, called vargas, split every sign into smaller parts to study one area of life in detail. The Navamsa (D9) is the best known and is read for marriage and the inner strength of planets. The Dashamsha (D10) is read for career, and the Saptamsha (D7) for children.

    How to read it

    Read the main chart first, then use a varga to look closer at one area. A planet that is strong in both the Rashi chart and the Navamsa is said to be in vargottama or well supported, and its results tend to be more reliable.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is a divisional chart?

    A chart made by dividing each sign into equal parts, which gives a more detailed view of one area of life.

    Which divisional chart is the most important?

    The Navamsa (D9), used for marriage and to judge planetary strength, along with the Rashi chart (D1).

    Does the birth time matter for vargas?

    Yes. Higher vargas such as D60 change within minutes, so use them only if your birth time is exact.
