    About Bharani

    Bharani is nakshatra number 2 of 27 and lies in Aries. Each nakshatra covers 13 degrees 20 minutes of the zodiac and is divided into four padas of 3 degrees 20 minutes.

    Bharani is the second nakshatra and covers the middle of Aries, from 13 degrees 20 minutes to 26 degrees 40 minutes. Its name comes from a root meaning to bear or carry, and its symbol is the yoni, the womb, a sign of bringing something through to completion. Yama, the god of dharma and death, presides over it.

    Venus rules Bharani, which gives a mix: Mars's sign pushes toward action, Yama's presence asks for restraint, and Venus brings desire and creativity. The nakshatra is often read as testing a person's ability to handle strong urges responsibly.

    The Moon in Bharani

    A Moon in Bharani tends to feel intensely and carry heavy responsibility without complaint. The person can be determined, disciplined and sensual at once. The shadow side is extremes: strong appetites, secrecy and an all-or-nothing attitude to duty and pleasure.

    Career

    Bharani suits work that handles endings and transitions: midwifery and obstetrics, law, accounting, the film and arts industries, funeral and hospice work, and any role carrying serious responsibility. It also fits people who manage money for others.

    Love and marriage

    In love, Bharani is passionate and loyal, though jealousy may follow strong attachment. It values honesty and expects a partner to carry their share. In Kundli matching it is a Manushya gana nakshatra.

    Questions about Bharani

    Who is the deity of Bharani?

    Yama, the lord of dharma and of the dead. He represents fairness, limits and the consequences of action.

    Is Bharani inauspicious?

    It is called a fierce (ugra) nakshatra and is traditionally avoided for gentle beginnings such as a housewarming. That describes a quality of timing, not the destiny of a person born under it.

    What does the Moon in Bharani mean for the Dasha?

    Your first Vimshottari Dasha is that of Venus, the lord of Bharani. How much of it remains at birth depends on how far the Moon has travelled through the nakshatra.
