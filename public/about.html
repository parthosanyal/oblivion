    What AstroSarangi is

    AstroSarangi is a free website for Vedic (Jyotish) astrology. You enter a date, time and place of birth and it calculates a Kundli: the birth chart, the planetary positions, the divisional charts, the Dasha periods and a set of readings built from them. It is meant for people who want to understand their chart, not only to read a one-line forecast.

    The tools run in your browser. Most of the calculation is done on your own device, and there is no sign-up or payment needed to use the main features.

    How the calculations work

    Planetary positions are calculated from astronomical formulae and ephemeris data, then converted to the sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa, the standard used in India. The main chart uses whole-sign houses, which is the classical Parashari method. A separate Krishnamurti Paddhati (KP) view uses Placidus cusps and sub-lords for those who follow that system.

    Dasha periods are calculated from the Moon's exact nakshatra position. The default is Vimshottari, with other systems available for comparison. Every output is a calculation from the birth data you enter, so the accuracy of the result depends on the accuracy of the birth time and place.

    What the Learn guides are

    The guides in the Learn section explain Vedic astrology in plain language: the twelve signs first, then nakshatras, planets, houses and Dasha. They are written for AstroSarangi and follow the classical Parashari approach. They are meant to help you read your own chart with more understanding.

    These guides give traditional meanings and tendencies. They are not predictions about any individual, since a real reading always depends on the whole chart.

    What astrology can and cannot do

    Astrology is a traditional system of symbolism, practised in India for many centuries. Many people find it useful for reflection and for choosing how to approach their lives. It is not a science in the modern sense, and there is no scientific evidence that it predicts events.

    Nothing on this site is medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. Please do not make major health, money or legal decisions on the basis of a chart, and see a qualified professional for those.
