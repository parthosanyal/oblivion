    What Virgo stands for

    Virgo, or Kanya, is the sixth sign, symbolised by the Maiden holding grain. It is an earth sign of the dual type, so it is practical but adaptable. Its traditional body area is the digestive system and intestines.

    Mercury rules Virgo, as it does Gemini. In Gemini Mercury shows its curious, talkative side. In Virgo it shows the analytic, critical and organising side. The sign is the natural sixth house, which covers daily work, service, health and routine.

    Virgo notices what is wrong, and it wants to fix it. This is the source of its skill with detail and its habit of self-criticism.

    Sun, Moon and rising sign in Virgo

    The Sun in Virgo is read as a modest, hard-working nature that earns respect through competence instead of display. Authority comes from expertise. It is a neutral sign for the Sun.

    The Moon in Virgo gives a careful, analytic mind that worries easily and likes order. It spans the last three quarters of Uttara Phalguni, all of Hasta, and the first half of Chitra, so the birth Dasha is the Sun, Moon or Mars.

    Virgo rising makes Mercury the first-house lord. People often look neat and youthful, with refined features. Venus rules the second and ninth houses and Saturn the fifth and sixth houses, so the planets of money and fortune take on a particular colour here.

    Career and money

    Virgo suits work that rewards accuracy: medicine, nursing, pharmacy, accounting, auditing, editing, research, quality control, data analysis, nutrition and administration. Service roles, in which others are helped step by step, also fit.

    Virgo is careful with money and usually keeps good records. The tradition warns against worry that stops investment or action.

    Love and marriage

    In relationships Virgo shows love through helpful acts instead of grand words. It can be shy at first, and critical when anxious. A partner who is patient, appreciative and calm helps it relax.

    Traditionally Virgo pairs well with other earth signs, Taurus and Capricorn, and with water signs Cancer and Scorpio. The seventh house and the Moon's nakshatra tell more about real compatibility.

    Health tendencies

    Virgo governs the intestines and digestion, so gut health, nervous digestion and food sensitivities are the traditional associations. Worry itself is often named as the root of health complaints.

    Clean routines, regular meals and relaxation practice are the usual traditional suggestions.

    This is traditional symbolism for reflection only. It is not medical advice, so see a qualified doctor for any health concern.

    Virgo and the planets

    Mercury is in its own sign and also exalted here, strongest at 15 degrees, with its moolatrikona from 16 to 20 degrees. That makes Virgo the best sign for Mercury. Venus is debilitated, weakest at 27 degrees: Venus here is said to find it hard to enjoy things because Virgo looks for flaws.

    Other planets are neutral or friendly. Check house and aspects as well, because sign dignity alone does not decide results.

    The Sun-sign dates and the Western zodiac

    Because the Vedic zodiac is sidereal and about 24 degrees behind the Western one, the Sun is in Virgo (Kanya) from about Sep 17 to Oct 16. Most people born in those weeks have a Western Sun sign of Libra, and only those born in the first days, up to about Sep 23, would call themselves Virgo in the Western system. Your Vedic Sun sign, Moon sign and rising sign are best taken from a calculated chart, not from a date table.

    Questions people ask about Virgo

    Which planet rules Virgo in Vedic astrology?

    Mercury (Budha) rules Virgo and also Gemini.

    Why is Mercury so strong in Virgo?

    Mercury is both in its own sign and exalted in Virgo, a rare combination for a planet. It peaks at 15 degrees.

    What are the Vedic dates for Virgo?

    The Sun is in Virgo from about 17 September to 16 October in the sidereal system.

    Which nakshatras fall in Virgo?

    The last three quarters of Uttara Phalguni, all of Hasta, and the first half of Chitra.
