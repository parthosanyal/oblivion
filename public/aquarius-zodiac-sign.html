    What Aquarius stands for

    Aquarius, or Kumbha, is the eleventh sign, symbolised by the Water-bearer holding a pot. It is an air sign of the fixed type, which makes it consistent in its thinking and slow to change its views. The traditional body areas are the calves, ankles and circulation.

    Saturn rules Aquarius, as it does Capricorn. In Capricorn Saturn builds personal structure. In Aquarius Saturn works at the level of groups, networks and systems. The sign is the natural eleventh house, the house of gains, friends, large organisations and the fulfilment of wishes.

    Aquarius is thoughtful, principled and often unconventional. It cares about the group and can be emotionally distant with individuals. Friendship is its natural language.

    Strengths

    • Original thinking
    • Humanitarian outlook
    • Loyalty in friendship
    • Calm under pressure

    Cautions

    • Emotional distance
    • Stubborn opinions
    • Rebellion for its own sake
    • Neglecting personal needs

    Sun, Moon and rising sign in Aquarius

    The Sun in Aquarius is read as a character that draws authority from ideas and from service to a wider group. Saturn is not the Sun's friend, so a personal sense of identity may take time to develop.

    The Moon in Aquarius gives a detached, observant mind that values independence. It spans the last half of Dhanishta, all of Shatabhisha, and the first three quarters of Purva Bhadrapada, so the birth Dasha is Mars, Rahu or Jupiter.

    Aquarius rising makes Saturn the first-house lord. People tend to be tall and lean with a thoughtful look. Venus rules the fourth and ninth houses and is a yogakaraka for this chart, giving a strong link between home, fortune and the intellect.

    Career and money

    Aquarius suits work with systems and large groups: technology, science, engineering, social work, networks, research, aviation, electronics, data, public welfare and reform movements. Teamwork and innovation fit it better than strict hierarchy.

    Gains often come through friends, communities and networks. The eleventh house nature of the sign means income can arrive from several sources at once.

    Love and marriage

    In relationships Aquarius is loyal but needs space. It is friendly and open-minded, and values partnership based on shared interests and mutual respect. Emotional expression can seem reserved to a partner who wants more warmth.

    Traditionally Aquarius pairs well with other air signs, Gemini and Libra, and with fire signs Aries and Sagittarius. Look at the Moon's nakshatra and the seventh house for the real picture.

    Health tendencies

    Aquarius governs the calves, ankles and circulation, so varicose veins, cramps, ankle strain and nervous tension are the traditional associations. Saturn adds slow, cold or stiff complaints.

    Regular walking, hydration and rest from screens and overthinking are the usual suggestions.

    This is traditional symbolism for reflection only. It is not medical advice, so see a qualified doctor for any health concern.

    Aquarius and the planets

    Saturn is at home and has its moolatrikona in Aquarius from 0 to 20 degrees. Mercury and Venus are friendly. The Sun and Moon are not in their favoured positions, since Aquarius is a sign of the Sun's enemy and the Moon's neutral. Many traditions assign Rahu as a co-ruler of Aquarius.

    No classical planet is exalted or debilitated in Aquarius.

    The Sun-sign dates and the Western zodiac

    Because the Vedic zodiac is sidereal and about 24 degrees behind the Western one, the Sun is in Aquarius (Kumbha) from about Feb 13 to Mar 13. Most people born in those weeks have a Western Sun sign of Pisces, and only those born in the first days, up to about Feb 19, would call themselves Aquarius in the Western system. Your Vedic Sun sign, Moon sign and rising sign are best taken from a calculated chart, not from a date table.

    Questions people ask about Aquarius

    Which planet rules Aquarius in Vedic astrology?

    Saturn (Shani) rules Aquarius, and many traditions also give Rahu a co-lordship.

    Is Aquarius a water sign?

    No. Despite the Water-bearer symbol, Aquarius is an air sign. The water is carried in the pot, not the nature of the sign.

    What are the Vedic dates for Aquarius?

    The Sun is in Aquarius from about 13 February to 13 March in the sidereal system.

    Which nakshatras fall in Aquarius?

    The last half of Dhanishta, all of Shatabhisha, and the first three quarters of Purva Bhadrapada.
