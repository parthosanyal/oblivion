    What Taurus stands for

    Taurus, or Vrishabha, is the second sign and its symbol is the Bull. It is an earth sign of the fixed type, which means it holds and sustains whatever Aries began. Where Aries starts, Taurus stays. The traditional body area is the face, throat and neck.

    Venus rules Taurus, the planet of pleasure, art, money, comfort and relationships. This gives the sign a reputation for loving good food, music, textiles and a stable home. A strong Taurus influence is read as patient, practical and loyal, with a strong need for security.

    The fixed quality has a second side. Taurus resists change, and once it has made up its mind it can be hard to move. Texts describe this as steadiness when it is useful and stubbornness when it is not.

    Strengths

    • Reliability and loyalty
    • Good sense for money and value

    • Overindulgence in comfort

    Sun, Moon and rising sign in Taurus

    The Sun in Taurus is in a neutral position: not exalted, not weak. It is read as a steady, determined character that works slowly toward material goals. Pride is tied to possessions and achievements that can be seen.

    The Moon is exalted in Taurus, strongest at 3 degrees, and it has its moolatrikona here from 4 to 30 degrees. This is the most comfortable sign for the mind. A Taurus Moon is calm, sensual and attached to home, food and family. The Moon sits in the last three quarters of Krittika, all of Rohini, and the first half of Mrigashira, so the starting Dasha is the Sun, Moon or Mars.

    With Taurus rising, Venus rules the first house. People are often described as having a pleasant face, a strong neck and a calm manner. Saturn is a yogakaraka for this ascendant because it rules the ninth and tenth houses, so a well-placed Saturn can give strong career results in mid-life.

    Career and money

    Taurus suits work connected to money, beauty and the senses: banking, finance, agriculture, hospitality, food, music, fashion, interior design, jewellery and luxury goods. It also suits long-term roles where reliability counts more than speed.

    Financially, Taurus is considered one of the better signs for saving. The risk is clinging to a failing plan for too long, so look for a trusted adviser when change is needed.

    Love and marriage

    In love Taurus is affectionate, physical and loyal. It moves slowly but commits deeply, and it expects the same loyalty back. Jealousy and possessiveness are the usual weak spots.

    Traditionally Taurus pairs well with other earth signs, Virgo and Capricorn, and with water signs Cancer and Pisces. As with every sign, a real compatibility check needs the Moon nakshatras and the seventh house of both charts.

    Health tendencies

    Taurus rules the throat and neck, so the thyroid, voice, tonsils and neck stiffness are the usual associations. A love of food means weight and sugar balance are traditional cautions.

    Walking, regular meals and keeping the voice rested are the common suggestions in traditional literature.

    This is traditional symbolism for reflection only. It is not medical advice, so see a qualified doctor for any health concern.

    Taurus and the planets

    Venus is in its own sign here and is strong. The Moon is exalted. Sun, Mercury and Saturn are friendly or neutral. Mars is neutral to uncomfortable, since Mars prefers quick action while Taurus favours patience. Ketu is sometimes said to be exalted in Taurus and Rahu in Scorpio, but Rahu and Ketu dignity differs between traditions.

    No classical planet is debilitated in Taurus. As always, check the whole chart: house, aspects and the Navamsa tell more than sign alone.

    The Sun-sign dates and the Western zodiac

    Because the Vedic zodiac is sidereal and about 24 degrees behind the Western one, the Sun is in Taurus (Vrishabha) from about May 15 to Jun 14. Most people born in those weeks have a Western Sun sign of Gemini, and only those born in the first days, up to about May 21, would call themselves Taurus in the Western system. Your Vedic Sun sign, Moon sign and rising sign are best taken from a calculated chart, not from a date table.

    Questions people ask about Taurus

    Which planet rules Taurus in Vedic astrology?

    Venus (Shukra) rules Taurus. It also rules Libra.

    Why is the Moon so strong in Taurus?

    The Moon is exalted in Taurus. Classical texts give Taurus as the Moon's sign of highest strength, with the peak at 3 degrees, and its moolatrikona covers the rest of the sign.

    What are the Vedic dates for Taurus?

    The Sun is in Taurus from about 15 May to 14 June in the sidereal system.

    Which nakshatras fall in Taurus?

    The last three quarters of Krittika, all of Rohini, and the first half of Mrigashira.
