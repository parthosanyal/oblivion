    Questions people ask

    Which ayanamsa and house system do you use?

    The main chart uses the Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa with whole-sign houses. The KP pages use the KP ayanamsa and Placidus house cusps, as KP requires.

    Why does my birth time matter?

    The Lagna changes about every two hours, and house-based readings depend on it. The Moon sign and nakshatra are usually stable within a few hours, so a rough time still gives useful Moon-based results.

    Is the calculation accurate?

    Planetary positions are accurate to a fraction of a degree, which is enough for charts and dashas. Check against a professional ephemeris for anything critical.

    Do you store my birth details?

    No. The calculations run in your browser and your birth date and time are not sent to our server. The last entry is remembered only in your own browser.

    What is KP astrology?

    Krishnamurti Paddhati judges events through house cusp sub-lords, significators and dasha periods. Open the KP section after creating your Kundli to see them.

    Is this a substitute for an astrologer?

    No. Readings are rule-based and meant for learning and reflection, not for medical, legal or financial decisions.
