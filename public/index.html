About Jyotish Kundli

Jyotish Kundli is a free Vedic astrology (Jyotish) birth chart generator. Enter your date, time and place of birth to get your Lagna, Rashi, Nakshatra and planetary positions using the Lahiri ayanamsa and whole-sign houses.

  • Lagna and Navamsa charts, plus every divisional chart from D1 to D60, in North and South Indian styles
  • Vimshottari and Yogini Dasha with Antardasha dates and a lifetime outlook timeline
  • Shadbala planetary strength with percentages, and Sarvashtakavarga and Bhinnashtakavarga charts
  • Panchang, yogas and doshas (Manglik, Kaal Sarp, Gajakesari and more), current transits and Sade Sati

All calculations run in your browser. Your birth details are not sent to any server. Results are for learning and reflection, not professional advice.
