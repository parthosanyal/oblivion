    About the career & finance

    Career in Vedic astrology is read from the 10th house, its lord, the Sun and Saturn, and the Navamsa and Dashamsha. Money is read from the 2nd and 11th houses and the combinations known as Dhana yogas.

    This report summarises work style by sign, fields that suit your chart, whether job, business or freelance fits better, savings and expenses, wealth yogas and the next ten years of Dasha for work and finance.

    What you get

    • Job, business or freelance guidance
    • Savings, assets and spending tendencies
    • Wealth yogas found in your chart
    • A ten-year career and finance outlook

    How to read it

    Look first at the 10th lord and its strength, then at the Dasha. Periods of the 10th lord or planets in the 10th, 2nd and 11th are the usual times for progress. The report gives tendencies to plan with, not guarantees.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which house shows career?

    The 10th house, with its lord, the Sun and Saturn.

    What is Dhana yoga?

    A combination connecting the lords of houses linked with wealth, such as 1, 2, 5, 9 and 11.

    Can the report pick my exact job?

    No. It suggests areas that fit your chart, not a single job.
