    What Libra stands for

    Libra, or Tula, is the seventh sign, symbolised by the Scales, usually held by a trader. It is an air sign of the movable type, so it starts activity through contact with other people. The traditional body areas are the kidneys and lower back.

    Venus rules Libra, as it does Taurus. In Taurus Venus shows possession and comfort. In Libra it shows partnership, art and diplomacy. The sign is the natural seventh house, the house of marriage, contracts and open dealings with the public.

    The scales explain the character: Libra weighs, compares and looks for fairness. That makes it a good mediator, and also an indecisive one when every option seems equal.

    Sun, Moon and rising sign in Libra

    The Sun is debilitated in Libra, weakest at 10 degrees. This is read as authority that depends on the opinion of others, with a tendency to defer or to seek approval. The weakness can be reduced when the Sun's dispositor Venus is strong, or by cancellation rules.

    The Moon in Libra gives a refined, sociable mind that feels uneasy in conflict and unsettled when alone. It spans the last two quarters of Chitra, all of Swati, and the first three quarters of Vishakha, so the birth Dasha is Mars, Rahu or Jupiter.

    Libra rising makes Venus the first-house lord. People are often described as graceful and pleasant, with an attractive face. Saturn rules the fourth and fifth houses and so is a yogakaraka, one of the strongest planets in this chart, giving results through steady effort.

    Career and money

    Libra suits work involving other people and fairness: law, diplomacy, counselling, design, fashion, beauty, art, public relations, trade, hospitality and partnerships. The scale is also the symbol of commerce, so business with a partner is common.

    With money, Libra enjoys comfort and style, and income often improves through partnerships or through a spouse. Decisions about large purchases can take a long time.

    Love and marriage

    Relationships matter greatly to Libra. It is charming, considerate and romantic, and wants an equal partner. The usual difficulty is avoiding conflict until it grows, and giving in too quickly to keep peace.

    Traditionally Libra pairs well with other air signs, Gemini and Aquarius, and with fire signs Leo and Sagittarius. The seventh house and the Moon's nakshatra still tell more than the sign.

    Health tendencies

    Libra rules the kidneys, lower back and skin, so urinary health, back ache and skin sensitivity are the traditional associations. Balance in diet, sugar and water intake is the usual advice.

    Venus also relates to the reproductive system and to hormonal balance.

    This is traditional symbolism for reflection only. It is not medical advice, so see a qualified doctor for any health concern.

    Libra and the planets

    Venus is in its own sign and has its moolatrikona from 0 to 15 degrees, so Venus is very comfortable here. Saturn is exalted, strongest at 20 degrees, because Saturn's sense of justice and discipline suits the sign of balance. The Sun is debilitated, weakest at 10 degrees.

    Mercury is friendly, the Moon and Jupiter are mixed, and Mars is uneasy since Mars prefers decisive action to weighing options.

    The Sun-sign dates and the Western zodiac

    Because the Vedic zodiac is sidereal and about 24 degrees behind the Western one, the Sun is in Libra (Tula) from about Oct 17 to Nov 15. Most people born in those weeks have a Western Sun sign of Scorpio, and only those born in the first days, up to about Oct 23, would call themselves Libra in the Western system. Your Vedic Sun sign, Moon sign and rising sign are best taken from a calculated chart, not from a date table.

    Questions people ask about Libra

    Which planet rules Libra in Vedic astrology?

    Venus (Shukra) rules Libra and Taurus.

    Why is Saturn exalted in Libra?

    Classical texts place Saturn's exaltation in Libra, at 20 degrees. The usual explanation is that Saturn is the planet of justice and Libra is the sign of balanced judgement.

    What are the Vedic dates for Libra?

    The Sun is in Libra from about 17 October to 15 November in the sidereal system.

    Which nakshatras fall in Libra?

    The last two quarters of Chitra, all of Swati, and the first three quarters of Vishakha.
